Type something, and Signboard shows it full screen, as big as it fits. That's the whole app.

Good for:
- Showing your hotel's address to a taxi driver, or a name on an airport pickup sign.
- Telling your address, phone number, social security number on your travels when your Mandarin is just not there yet to do this verbally at speed.
- Talking with someone who's deaf or hard of hearing, or with anyone at a loud concert or bar.
- Silently shouting "Go aunt Amalia!" on your tablet at racing events / protests / funerals.
- Cheating at tests.
- Dating, probably.

Features:
- Big full-screen text with auto-sizing.
- Handles portrait-landscape transitions.
- Screen stays on while app is open.
- White-on-black/black-on-white mode toggle.
- Quick access to your recent 5 texts.
- Handles notches and camera holes.
- 100% local. No ads, no internet, no tracking, no permissions.
- Tiny: the whole app is ~30 KB.
- Open source: https://github.com/vdavid/signboard

Long-press the screen anywhere to edit the text.
